AlertTriage

AI-powered analysis of security alerts to identify false positives and reduce the noise that buries your SOC team. Works with any LLM model and learns from your environment over time to improve accuracy.

  • Analyzes alerts using Claude, GPT, or your preferred LLM
  • Learns from analyst feedback to improve over time
  • Reduces false positive workload by 30-40% on average
  • Privacy-first architecture with optional anonymization
  • Integrates with Splunk, ELK, and other SIEM platforms

Evadex

Test your DLP scanner's real-world effectiveness by generating hundreds of obfuscated variants of sensitive data and measuring what gets detected. Helps you find gaps in your detection coverage before attackers do.

  • Generates 500+ obfuscation variants of sensitive values
  • Tests through real file formats (DOCX, PDF, XLSX, etc.)
  • Tests 13 different evasion technique families
  • Works with any scanner that has a CLI interface
  • Detailed reports showing detection gaps and coverage
